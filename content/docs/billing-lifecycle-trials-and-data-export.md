CuspFlow is designed so you're never cut off abruptly and your data is always yours. Here's the full lifecycle.

## The free trial

Every new clinic starts with a fully-featured **30-day free trial** — the entire platform, no credit card required. You only add payment details when you're ready to subscribe. During the trial you can add as many staff as you like; you'll choose a [plan](/docs/account-subscription/choosing-and-managing-your-subscription) as the trial ends.

## If a payment lapses

If a payment fails, you aren't locked out straight away:

1. **Quiet retries.** Everything keeps working while the card is retried, and only the Owner and Administrator see a discreet notice.
2. **View-only window.** If it still fails, the clinic enters a **60-day view-only window**. Every record stays readable and fully exportable, but new appointments, charting, uploads and checkout pause until billing is fixed.
3. **Reactivate any time.** Fixing billing restores everything instantly, including any locked staff.
4. **Final deletion.** Only if nobody returns within those 60 days is the data finally deleted — with reminders sent along the way.

## Export all your data — free, any time

At any point, **Settings → Billing** offers a free, complete export of everything: patients, medical history, appointments, treatment plans, billing and payments. There are no restrictions and no need to ask. During a view-only window the same full export stays available, so you can always take your data with you.

## Security & privacy

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each clinic's data is completely isolated from every other clinic. CuspFlow follows HIPAA-aligned security practices. See the [FAQ](/docs/reference/faq) for more.
