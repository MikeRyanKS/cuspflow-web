Every treatment plan has exactly **one invoice**. It's created at the first checkout and tracks the line items, subtotal, any discount, how much has been paid, and the remaining balance.

## Taking a payment

When the patient is ready to pay, open the **checkout** view from the treatment plan (or move them to the Billing stage in the [Waiting Room](/docs/patients-scheduling/waiting-room)):

1. **Review the line items** — procedures, prescriptions and any [lab charges](/docs/clinical/lab-cases).
2. **Apply a discount** if needed — a flat amount or a percentage. A discount can never exceed the subtotal.
3. **Record the payment** — enter the amount and choose a method.
4. **Print the receipt.**

## Payment methods

Cash, Card, Mobile Money, Insurance and Waived.

## Finalising and discharge

An invoice can be **finalised** once the balance is cleared. Finalising is what allows the patient to be **discharged** — the Waiting Room won't let a patient leave until their bill is finalised. Balances can never go negative.

## Money in your currency

The currency symbol, decimal format and locale all come from your clinic's [settings](/docs/getting-started/initial-clinic-setup) — nothing is hardcoded — so your receipts and payments always show your own currency and number formatting.

## Paying over time?

Patients don't have to pay it all at once. See [Installments & receipts](/docs/billing-payments/installments-and-receipts) for partial payments and receipt formats.
