The HR module manages your workforce. It's built on a deliberate split between a person's **HR profile** and their **login**.

## Staff records vs. logins

- A **staff record** is an HR profile, name, role, salary, qualifications. It can exist on its own.
- A **user** is a staff member who has also been granted a system login. Granting access sends an email invitation.

So a staff member can have a full HR record long before, or without, being given a login. Not everyone on the payroll needs to sign in.

## Adding a staff member

From **HR → Directory**, add a person with their details and a [role](/docs/core-concepts/roles-and-permissions): Owner, Administrator, Finance, HR, Receptionist, Doctor, Nurse or Support Staff. Set their branch and remuneration (base salary, hourly rate, commission rate). Optionally grant a login, which emails them an invitation to set a password.

Each staff member's drawer has tabs for personal info, remuneration, qualifications, system access and documents.

## Roles and custom permissions

A person's role controls what they can see and do. Where someone needs a little more, an Owner or Administrator can grant **custom permission overrides** under **Settings → Staff Permissions**. Only the Owner can change a staff member's role. See [Roles & permissions](/docs/core-concepts/roles-and-permissions) for the full model.

## Staff schedules

Set each person's weekly working hours per branch under **HR → Staff Schedules**. These hours drive [appointment availability](/docs/patients-scheduling/appointments), so the booking form only offers slots when a doctor is actually working.
