When a treatment needs external laboratory work — crowns, dentures, bridges, aligners — create a **lab case** linked to the treatment plan. Lab cases live under **Lab Cases**, or can be created directly from a [treatment plan](/docs/clinical/treatment-plans).

## Your laboratory directory

CuspFlow keeps a directory of your partner labs (name, contact, address, status). Add your labs once and reuse them across cases.

## The lifecycle

Each lab case records the assigned lab, the required doctor, the case type, the teeth involved (FDI codes) and the dates through its lifecycle:

**Draft → Sent → In Production → Received → Fitted** (or Cancelled)

Track the key dates as the case moves along, so nothing sits forgotten at the lab.

## Costs on both sides

A lab case tracks money in two directions:

- **What the lab charges you** — the lab's invoice cost, plus transport and other costs, with a lab payment status of **Pending, Partial** or **Paid**.
- **What the patient is charged** — one or more procedure line items; the patient charge is their sum.

## Automatic billing and teeth sync

When a lab case is linked to a treatment plan, CuspFlow automatically:

- **Adds it as a billing line item** on that plan's invoice, and
- **Merges the case's teeth codes** into the plan's teeth chart.

So the treatment plan and the invoice always stay in sync with the lab work — you don't enter it twice.
