A **treatment plan** is the complete record of a single visit. It's created automatically when a patient is checked in — from a booking or a walk-in — and stays on file permanently. Open it from the patient's [Waiting Room](/docs/patients-scheduling/waiting-room) card or their profile.

## What a plan holds

- Every **procedure** performed (root canal, extraction, cleaning, and so on).
- Every **prescription** issued (medicines, gels, products).
- **Clinical notes** from the dentist or nurse.
- **X-rays and images** taken during the visit.
- The **invoice** for the visit.
- **Signed consent forms**.
- The **care team** — which staff worked on the plan.
- The patient's **journey** timings.

## The teeth chart

The teeth chart is an interactive **FDI** diagram where the clinician marks which teeth are involved and can add per-tooth notes. (See the [Glossary](/docs/core-concepts/glossary) for how FDI numbering works.)

## Adding items and notes

Add procedures and prescriptions from your [price catalog](/docs/operations/price-catalog), or enter them manually. Write clinical notes directly, or insert a reusable [note template](/docs/clinical/treatment-and-note-templates) and edit from there.

## Pricing is locked in one direction

Prices flow from the catalog into the plan. Once an item is added and payment begins, **that item's price is locked** — like a supermarket checkout. Dosages and clinical notes can still be edited, but prices can't change mid-visit or after checkout. Updating the catalog only affects future visits.

## The care team

Each plan needs a **Main Doctor** — checkout is blocked until one is assigned. Assisting staff are optional. Anyone who adds an item, changes status or takes payment is automatically recorded as a contributor, which drives clinical accountability and [commission](/docs/staff-hr/payroll) calculations.

## Status

A plan is **Ongoing** while the visit is active, and becomes **Finalized** or **Cancelled**. The invoice has its own separate paid/finalised state — see [Checkout & taking payment](/docs/billing-payments/checkout-and-taking-payment).

## The Treatment Plans list

The **Treatment Plans** item in the sidebar is a clinic-wide view of every plan, newest first — searchable by patient and filterable by treatment status, payment status and doctor. Open any row to jump straight into that plan.

## Needs a lab?

If the treatment involves external lab work, create a [lab case](/docs/clinical/lab-cases) from the plan — it links back automatically and adds itself to the invoice.
