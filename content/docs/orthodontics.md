Orthodontic treatment runs over many months, so it's tracked separately from a single visit. **Ortho cases** live on the patient's profile, giving you a running view of where each patient is in their course of treatment.

## Case types

CuspFlow tracks the two common approaches:

- **Fixed braces**, record the bracket system and type, and the current wire in the sequence.
- **Clear aligners**, record the aligner brand, the current stage and the total number of stages.

## Tracking progress

Each ortho case records:

- **Status**, Active, On Hold, Completed or Discontinued.
- **Start date** and **estimated end date**.
- **Progress notes** you add over time.

Because the case sits on the patient's profile, anyone reviewing that patient can see their orthodontic history at a glance alongside their [treatment plans](/docs/clinical/treatment-plans) and [medical history](/docs/patients-scheduling/patient-records).
