Families group related patients, a household, so you can manage shared contact details and see a combined balance. Each family has a **Head of Family** who acts as the billing and contact anchor.

## Creating a family

From **Families**, create a new family and add members, or add an existing patient to a family from their profile. One member is designated **Head of Family**; any member can be promoted to that role later.

## Why families matter

- **Shared contact channel.** Because the family provides a contact point, children under 18 can be registered without their own phone number as long as they belong to a family.
- **Household balance.** A patient's outstanding balance can be viewed at the family level, so you can see what a whole household owes rather than chasing each person separately.
- **Faster front desk.** Related patients are linked, so finding a child from a parent's record (or vice versa) is quick.

## Managing a family

On the family profile you can:

- See every member and their individual balances.
- View the combined outstanding balance.
- Promote a different member to Head of Family.

For following up on what a family owes, see [Overdue payments](/docs/billing-payments/overdue-payments).
