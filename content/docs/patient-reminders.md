CuspFlow can message your patients automatically about **upcoming appointments** and **overdue balances**, so the front desk doesn't have to chase them by hand. The **Patient Reminders** page (in the sidebar) is where you see what's queued and what's been sent.

## How messages are sent

Reminders go out over **WhatsApp**, with **email** as an automatic fallback when a patient can't be reached on WhatsApp.

You connect your clinic's own **WhatsApp Business** account once, under **Settings → WhatsApp**. Meta bills you for the messages directly — CuspFlow doesn't mark them up. (CuspFlow used to offer SMS; it was retired in favour of WhatsApp, which is cheaper and more reliable across the regions we serve.)

## Choosing what goes out

Under **Settings → Notifications** you choose which reminders are active and their timing — for example, an appointment reminder the day before, and an overdue-balance nudge a set number of days after the invoice.

## Excluding a patient

Some patients don't want automated messages. You can **exclude an individual patient** from all reminders — from the Patient Reminders page or from the patient's own profile — without affecting anyone else. Reminders are on for the whole clinic by default; the exclusion list is the opt-out.
