Everything about how your documents print lives in one place: **Settings → Print & Receipts**. From here you choose paper sizes and control exactly what appears on your receipts, with a live preview that updates as you edit.

## Paper formats

Each document type has its own paper size, because a receipt printer, a certificate and a paystub don't always share the same tray:

- **Receipts** — A4, half-page A5, or narrow thermal till-roll.
- **Medical Forms**, **Prescriptions**, **Payroll Paystubs** — A4 or A5 each.

All default to A4. Change any of them independently if that document prints on a different size at your clinic.

The same page also holds the **Offline Record Printing** toggle, which adds a "Print Full Record" button to each patient profile (always A4).

## Branding your receipt

Below the paper formats you build the receipt's header and footer. A preview on the right shows the result at A4, A5 and thermal as you type, so you can see exactly what will print before you save.

### Header

Kept deliberately compact so the layout stays clean:

- **Logo, branch name and branch address** — toggle each on or off. The branch name and address come from the branch where the payment was taken.
- **Extra address line** — one short line under the address, for example "Opposite City Building".
- **Phone numbers** — up to four. If you leave these empty, the receipt falls back to the branch's own phone number.
- **Emails** — up to two.

### Footer

The footer has more room:

- **Thank-you note** — a short message to the patient.
- **Other branches** — contact details for your clinic's other locations.
- **Opening hours**.
- **Promotional / info note** — anything you want patients to see, for example a seasonal offer.
- **Legal disclaimer** — printed in its own bordered box at the bottom, for example a returns or tax note.
- **"Printed by" line** — show or hide the staff member who printed the receipt.

Only the fields you fill in appear on the receipt, so an empty footer stays clean.

## Where else this appears

Your header and footer settings are used everywhere a document is produced, so your branding is consistent:

- The **printed receipt** (and the receipt's live preview).
- The **emailed receipt PDF** — a patient can be sent their receipt by email, and it uses the same header and footer.
- **Prescriptions** and **medical forms** show the same clinic header and a contact/opening-hours/disclaimer footer (they don't show the thank-you or promotional note, since those are for receipts).

## Receipt numbering

Receipt numbers are assigned automatically in a gap-free sequence per clinic, using the prefix you set under [Settings → General](/docs/getting-started/initial-clinic-setup) (for example `RCP-`, producing `RCP-00001`).
