CuspFlow is a cloud practice-management system for dental clinics. It follows a patient from the moment they walk in — registration, appointment, waiting room, treatment, billing and payment — and gives owners the reporting and staff-management tools to run the business. It works across one clinic or many branches, and each clinic's data is completely private to that clinic.

This guide is a map of the whole system. Skim it once, then follow the links into the detailed how-tos for anything you want to do.

## The patient journey, end to end

Most of what happens in a clinic follows one path, and CuspFlow is built around it:

1. **Register the patient** — create a [patient record](/docs/patients-scheduling/patient-records) with their contact and medical details.
2. **Book or check in** — schedule an [appointment](/docs/patients-scheduling/appointments), or create a walk-in on the spot.
3. **Track the visit** — checked-in patients appear on the live [Waiting Room](/docs/patients-scheduling/waiting-room) board.
4. **Record treatment** — open the [treatment plan](/docs/clinical/treatment-plans) to chart procedures, notes and X-rays.
5. **Take payment** — [check out](/docs/billing-payments/checkout-and-taking-payment), record the payment and print a receipt.
6. **Discharge** — once the bill is finalised, the patient leaves the board.

## Setting up your clinic

If you've just signed up, do these first:

- **[Initial clinic setup](/docs/getting-started/initial-clinic-setup)** — branches, dental chairs and your first staff members.
- **[Import your existing data](/docs/getting-started/importing-your-data)** — bring patients, appointments and inventory over from a spreadsheet or another system.
- **[Configure the price catalog](/docs/operations/price-catalog)** — the list of procedures and prescriptions you bill for.

## Who does what

Access is controlled by **role** — Owner, Administrator, Finance, HR, Receptionist, Doctor, Nurse or Support Staff. Each role sees only the parts of the system it needs. See [Roles & permissions](/docs/core-concepts/roles-and-permissions) for the full breakdown.

## Learn the vocabulary

CuspFlow uses some dental and practice-management terms consistently — FDI tooth numbering, treatment plan, Bill of Materials, view-only window and more. The [Glossary](/docs/core-concepts/glossary) defines them all in one place.

## Where things live

| You want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Find or add a patient | Patients |
| Book or check in a patient | Appointments |
| Let patients request appointments online | Settings → Online Booking (review in the Reception dashboard) |
| Manage appointment / balance reminders | Patient Reminders |
| See who's in the clinic now | Waiting Room |
| Record treatment, notes, X-rays | Treatment plan |
| Review every treatment plan in the clinic | Treatment Plans |
| Order crown/denture work | Lab Cases |
| Take a payment / print a receipt | Checkout (inside the treatment plan) |
| Track an insurance claim | Financials → Insurance Claims |
| See revenue and outstanding balances | Financials |
| Track stock and low-stock alerts | Inventory |
| Manage staff, pay and attendance | HR |
| Restore something deleted | Settings → Trash |
| Configure the clinic | Settings |
| Manage your CuspFlow subscription | Settings → Billing |
