The **price catalog** is the list of procedures and prescriptions your clinic bills for. It's what feeds line items into treatment plans and invoices. Manage it under **Settings → Price Catalog** (also reachable as **Price Catalog** in the sidebar).

## Adding catalog items

Create an item with a **name** and **price**. Items can be procedures (fillings, extractions, crown prep) or prescriptions (medicines, gels, products). You can edit or remove items at any time.

## Prices lock at checkout

Editing a catalog item only affects **future** visits. Once an item has been added to a plan and payment has begun, its price is locked on that plan — so changing your catalog never rewrites a patient's existing bill. See [Treatment plans](/docs/clinical/treatment-plans) for how price-locking works.

## Bill of Materials

For items that consume stock, add a **Bill of Materials (BOM)** that links the catalog item to one or more [inventory](/docs/operations/inventory) items. When the catalog item is billed on a treatment plan, CuspFlow automatically deducts the linked stock. For example, a whitening procedure might deduct a gel sachet and a pair of gloves.

Keeping catalog and inventory linked this way means your stock levels stay accurate without anyone having to remember to deduct supplies by hand.
