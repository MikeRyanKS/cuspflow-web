Where patients are covered by insurance, CuspFlow helps you track claims from submission to outcome. Claims live under **Financials → Insurance Claims**.

## Raising a claim

Create a claim against the patient with the **carrier**, **policy number** and the **amount claimed**. The Insurance Claims screen shows a running total of what's been claimed, approved and rejected across the clinic, plus a per-carrier breakdown.

## The claim lifecycle

Each claim moves through:

**Draft → Submitted → Approved** (or **Rejected**) **→ Paid**

so you always know which claims are still with the carrier, which have been settled, and which have actually been paid out. Filter the list by status, or group it by carrier.

## The shortfall

When a claim is **Approved** for less than the amount claimed, record the **approved amount** — CuspFlow shows the **shortfall** the patient still owes, so it doesn't get lost. Print or export the claims report to send to a carrier.

## Carriers and policies

- Maintain a directory of **insurance carriers** under **Settings → Insurance**.
- Record each patient's **policy details** on their profile, so claims carry the right carrier and policy information.

## Insurance as a payment method

At [checkout](/docs/billing-payments/checkout-and-taking-payment), **Insurance** is one of the available payment methods, so the portion covered by a claim is recorded alongside any cash or card payment the patient makes directly.

Insurance claim outcomes and outstanding amounts feed into your [financial reports](/docs/reference/reports-and-analytics).
